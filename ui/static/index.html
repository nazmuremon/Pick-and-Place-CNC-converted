    Keys: arrows = move X / Y, [ ] = Z 0.1, { } = Z 1, , . = turn 10 deg, h = Home, space = STOP. Mouse: click the picture or the map = go there.

    0. Bed survey (all labels, camera high)

    Z at the TOP (a wide view), HOME in the picture. The head goes only to the front and to the left, in short steps checked by the camera, and to the front only until PCB 1 or PCB 2 shows. It measures HOME, the slots and PCB 1-4, puts the board area between the PCB labels, and saves the reach it drove.

    1. Reach (the 4 ends)

    The machine has no limit switches. Press Start, drive slowly to the left, right, front and back ends (stop about 5 mm before each end), then Save.

    2. Camera height (Z0)

    Move Z until the nozzle tip is about 5 mm above the parts and the board. Then press the button (once, the first time). Every height below is kept from this height, and Home brings Z back to it.

    4. Camera to nozzle

    a) Click a part in a slot (it goes to the red cross), press the button, then move the nozzle tip exactly over that part and press the button again = PICK offset.
    b) The same on one of the board's holes = PLACE offset.

    4b. Where the nozzle lands (best way)

    PICK: put the red cross over soft tape or a part in a slot, press "Land at pick height": the nozzle comes down there and leaves a mark. PLACE: pump ON with a part held, red cross over the board, press "Land at place height": the part is put down. Then CLICK the centre of the mark / the part in the picture.

    4c. Nozzle marks with ink (tip at every servo angle, runout)

    An ink spot (paper wet from the stamp pad) and a clean white paper, both taped flat near slot 3. 1) Click the ink spot in the picture, press "Ink spot here". 2) The same for a clean place on the paper. 3) Jog the needle down until it just touches the paper: Setup 3, Paper, Set. 4) Make the marks.

    4d. Nozzle pointer by eye (precise)

    1) Press "Target: click it", then CLICK the centre of a part in the live picture (a magenta cross shows it). 2) "Nozzle over the target": the needle comes down to 0.5 mm above it. 3) LOOK at the needle and nudge it with the fine steps until it is exactly over the target (Z +-0.1 on the Machine tab helps to see). 4) Save.
