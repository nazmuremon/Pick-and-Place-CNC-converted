Bench wiring: vacuum pump and nozzle servo

Everything runs from the one Arduino Uno with the Protoneer CNC Shield V3.00. The Uno runs our Grbl build ($I shows 1.1h.20261007). Its spindle pin D11 sends the servo pulses: M3 S1500 means a 1500 µs pulse. The pump relay sits on the coolant pin: M8 on, M9 off. Grbl runs both only after the moves before them have finished. One buck converter set to 5.0 V feeds the MG995 270° servo and the relay module, so nothing takes power from the Uno; its ground is joined to the shield's.

The shield's labels come from Grbl 0.9. In Grbl 1.1 the spindle output is D11, which the shield prints as the Z end stop. Each end-stop place has a signal pin and a GND pin. The GND pin beeps against the shield's GND on a meter, so use the other one. Check where A4 comes out on your shield with a meter too, before you add the valve.

Before power-on

  1. Relay first, USB only. Without the pump supply, run tools/servo_pump_test.py --pump: the relay must click on for 3 s, then off. If it works the other way round, the module is active-LOW: set its jumper to H, or use another module.
  2. Set the buck converter to 5.0 V with a meter before the servo is plugged in.
  3. Capacitor at the servo plug, stripe (−) to GND.
  4. Join the grounds: buck OUT− to the shield's GND (the CoolEn row's pin at the board edge). Without it the servo twitches and the relay may not switch.
  5. Diode across the pump, stripe to the pump's +.
  6. Servo test: tools/servo_pump_test.py --servo sweeps it (centre, both ends, quarter points) and leaves it at the centre. --us 1500 holds one position, --off lets it go limp.

Never feed the servo from the Uno's 5 V pin. Its current peaks pull the 5 V down and reset the Uno, and then the head position is lost.

Still open

  • Pump voltage and current, which set the supply and the fuse.
  • Pulse width to nozzle angle, and the time the servo needs to turn. Until they are measured, the tools wait 1 s after each turn. The 270° range differs from servo to servo, so it is measured, not assumed.
  • The valve on A4, when you add it: the same relay wiring as the pump, with its own diode across the valve coil.
